# Hotel-Booking-Performance-Cancellation-Analysis
Analyzed hotel booking data to identify cancellation trends, booking patterns, customer behavior, and key business insights using SQL, Python, and Power BI.


## Business Background

Imagine you have been hired as a Data Analyst for a hotel chain that operates two types of properties: Resort Hotels and City Hotels.

The management team has identified several business challenges:

- A high number of bookings are cancelled before arrival, resulting in potential revenue loss.
- Hotel occupancy varies across different months, making resource planning difficult.
- Management lacks clear insights into the customer segments most likely to cancel bookings.
- Room pricing and deposit policies may affect booking and cancellation behavior.
- Marketing campaigns may not be effectively targeting the right customer segments.

The hotel management wants to use historical booking data to understand these challenges and make data-driven business decisions.

## Problem Statement

Hotel management is experiencing booking cancellations, inconsistent occupancy levels, and challenges in understanding customer booking behavior. Without proper analysis, it is difficult to identify cancellation patterns, improve revenue performance, and optimize hotel operations.

This project analyzes historical hotel booking data to identify the key factors influencing cancellations, evaluate booking trends, and uncover opportunities to improve occupancy and revenue management.

## Business Objectives

- Calculate the overall hotel booking cancellation rate.
- Identify the months with the highest and lowest booking volumes and cancellation rates.
- Compare booking performance between City Hotels and Resort Hotels.
- Analyze cancellation patterns by country, customer type, deposit type, and market segment.
- Investigate how lead time affects booking cancellations.
- Evaluate the relationship between room type, pricing, and cancellation behavior.
- Provide actionable recommendations to reduce cancellations and improve hotel performance.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Python:** Data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, and trend analysis.
- **Pandas and NumPy:** Data manipulation and preprocessing.
- **Matplotlib and Seaborn:** Data visualization.
- **SQL:** Querying booking data and calculating business KPIs.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and business reporting.
- **Jupyter Notebook:** Analysis documentation and execution.
